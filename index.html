Residential development across Newcastle and the Hunter.

Townhouse developments and land subdivisions.

We develop townhouses and subdivide land across Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, Maitland and the wider Hunter, and we often work directly with landowners.

What we do

Townhouse developments

Well-located sites in the established suburbs of Newcastle and Lake Macquarie, developed as townhouse projects.

Land subdivisions

Residential and rural land across the Hunter, subdivided into new home sites, from a single additional lot to a larger rural subdivision.

Our approach

01Site assessment

Before we acquire a site we assess the sales evidence for new homes in the area, the planning controls that apply, and the cost of servicing and building on the land.

02Approvals

We take each project through planning approval and subdivision, working with local consultants.

03Delivery

Construction is carried out by established local builders and managed by Elyndra through to completion.

For landowners

Whether you would consider selling all or part of your land, or would prefer to develop it with our help, we would welcome a short, confidential conversation at the property or by phone. We are happy to work around your circumstances and your timing, and there is no obligation of any kind.
